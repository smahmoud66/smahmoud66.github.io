About Me

Mohammad Ali Seyed Mahmoud is a Ph.D. student in the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Surya Kalidindi. He earned his M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran.

His research sits at the intersection of materials informatics, solid mechanics, and probabilistic modeling, with a focus on applying machine learning and Bayesian inference to materials characterization and damage modeling.

Research

Current Work: Inverse Framework for Small Punch Tests

Developing an inverse framework for high-throughput mechanical property identification from Small Punch Tests. The approach combines full-field Digital Image Correlation (DIC) measurements with finite element simulations, flow-based generative models, and uncertainty quantification to enable rapid, data-driven materials characterization from small-scale specimens.
